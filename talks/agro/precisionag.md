Why precision agriculture?
==========================

Farms increasingly combine field sensors, automation, and machine intelligence.

<!-- pause -->

The goal is not more technology for its own sake. The goal is better decisions with less waste.

<!-- end_slide -->

The field becomes observable
============================

<!-- incremental_lists: true -->

* Soil and weather sensors expose local conditions.
* Robots turn observations into repeatable action.
* Models connect measurements to timing and risk.

<!-- end_slide -->

<!-- column_layout: [1, 1] -->

<!-- column: 0 -->

* Plant health

Actions

Control matters
===============

> Technology trust is useful, but control is better.

Farmers should own the data paths, failure modes, and operational decisions around their tools.
